**Credit Risk Scorecard: Probability of Default Model** | Python, scikit-learn, pandas | [GitHub link]
- Built an end-to-end application scorecard on 149,999 borrowers: data cleaning, monotonic WoE binning, IV-based variable selection and logistic regression, scaled to a 600-point score (PDO 20).
- Achieved test AUC 0.853, KS 0.55 and Gini 0.71 (95% CI 0.845 to 0.860) with a train-test AUC gap of 0.000; verified calibration by decile and score stability with PSI (0.0003).
- Benchmarked against XGBoost (AUC 0.867): the interpretable scorecard gives up 0.015 AUC while staying fully explainable.
- Wrote reusable, unit-tested modules for WoE/IV, PSI and score scaling, and a cut-off analysis linking score to approval rate and portfolio bad rate.
